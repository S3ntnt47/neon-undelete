# Neon Undelete Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Neon Undelete is a file-recovery tool that runs entirely on your own computer.

**What the app accesses.** When you choose a drive and start a scan, the app reads that drive's file-system structures (the NTFS Master File Table and allocation bitmap) and, when you preview or recover an item, the contents of the deleted files you select. It opens drives read-only and does not change them.

**What the app stores.** The only files it writes are the ones you ask it to recover, in the destination folder you choose. It keeps no logs, history or analytics.

**What the app sends.** Nothing. Neon Undelete makes no network connections. No personal data, file names, file contents, usage statistics or diagnostics leave your device.

**Third parties.** None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
